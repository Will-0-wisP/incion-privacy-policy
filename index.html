Privacy Policy – Incion

Last updated: April 2026
Secure • AdMob Ready • Firebase Integrated

This Privacy Policy applies to the Incion mobile application (“the App”). The App allows users to create and manage text and audio notes securely.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Terms & Conditions

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Email address (via Firebase Authentication)

b) User Content

  • Text notes
  • Audio recordings

c) Device & Usage Data

  • Device type and OS
  • Usage behavior
  • IP address and advertising identifiers

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain app features
  • Enable login and syncing
  • Improve performance
  • Display ads

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (authentication & storage)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)

4. Advertising

We use AdMob to serve ads. These may use cookies and device identifiers to provide personalized and non-personalized ads.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone – record audio notes
  • Storage – save notes
  • Internet – app functionality and ads

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information is shared only with trusted services like Firebase and AdMob.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

8. User Control & Data Deletion

Users can manage and delete notes inside the app at any time. If you wish to delete your entire account and purge all associated storage records permanently, you can also submit a request directly through the web interface below. You do not need to have the app currently installed to use this link.

Account & Data Deletion Request

Initiating this request will permanently remove your Firebase Authentication profile, along with all synced text notes and recorded audio fragments.

Request Account Deletion via Email

Important: Please ensure you send the request from the email address registered inside the Incion App. Data purges are permanent, irreversible, and typically completed within 7 business days.
