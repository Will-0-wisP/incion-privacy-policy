Privacy Policy

  • Effective: October 2026
  • App: Incion for Android
  • Contact: support.minzio@gmail.com

Summary

  • Incion lets you create text and audio notes and sync them to your account.
  • We collect your email address, the notes and recordings you create, and basic device and usage data.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob. We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can delete notes at any time, or delete your whole account and data without needing the app installed.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Incion ("Incion", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Incion mobile application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Please also read our Terms & Conditions.

We do not access, read or listen to your notes and recordings except where you ask us for support or where required by law. We do not collect precise location, contacts, or payment card details through the App.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To store your notes and recordings and sync them to your account.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • To display advertising that helps keep the App available.
  • To respond to your requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

The App relies on the following service providers, which process data under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, database and file storage): Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob (advertising): Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising and your choices

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar technologies to show personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance.

  • On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path may vary by device).
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only:

  • with service providers that operate the App for us (Firebase and AdMob, see section 4);
  • when required by law, legal process or a valid government request;
  • to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the App or the public;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you of any change in how your data is handled.

8. Data retention

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or irreversibly remove your data as described in section 11. We may keep limited information longer only where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud.

9. Security

Data is sent between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and Google Cloud encrypts data stored in Firebase. Access to backend data is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Choose a strong, unique password and keep your device protected.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, the CCPA/CPRA in California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data (see section 11);
  • withdraw consent, for example to personalized ads or microphone access;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and request a copy of your data;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support.minzio@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

11. Account and data deletion

You can delete individual notes inside the App at any time. To delete your entire Incion account and all associated data, use either method below. You do not need the App installed.

Option A: Delete from inside the App

Open the App, go to your account settings and choose Delete account. Your account is removed immediately.

Option B: Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to support.minzio@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request – Incion. Send it from the email address registered to your Incion account.
  2. We may reply to confirm your identity. We will never ask for your password.
  3. We delete your account and data within 7 business days of verification and send you a confirmation email.
Request account deletion by email

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Export or copy any notes you want to keep before you request it.

12. International transfers

Our service providers operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including countries whose data protection laws differ from yours. Where required, such transfers rely on safeguards provided by Google, such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Children's privacy

Incion is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, email us and we will delete it promptly.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. For significant changes we will give notice in the App or by email. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
